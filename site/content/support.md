# Support

Pleione is made by one developer at Merope, in the open. Here is the fastest way to an answer.

## Before you write

- **No channels?** Pleione comes without any. Add your provider's Xtream account or M3U playlist
  in **Settings › Sources**; the [setup guide](/setup/) walks through it.
- **A channel will not play?** Check that it plays in another app with the same account: most
  playback problems are the stream's. In **Settings › Sources**, **Live stream format** switches
  an Xtream account between TS and HLS, which some providers handle differently.
- **The guide is empty or off by an hour?** Add a guide in **Settings › Program Guide**, or use
  **EPG time correction** there.
- **Movies and Series are missing?** They need an Xtream account; M3U playlists have Live only.
- **Forgot the parental PIN?** It cannot be recovered: **Settings › Data Management › Reset App**
  removes it, along with your sources and other data.

## Report a bug or ask for a feature

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/ahmedsaed/pleione-webos-iptv-player/issues). Say
which TV model and webOS version you have (both are in the TV's own settings), which version of
Pleione (Settings › Data Management, at the bottom), what
you did, and what happened. Please leave out your provider's address, username and password, and
any channel names: they are not needed to find a bug.

## Contact

support@merope.dev

any provider, and ships no content of its own.
